The LW Alliance Helper app

The LW Alliance Helper app works alongside the bot. Members get a plan for each Alliance Duel (VS) day, built from their own game profile. Leadership gets scores, leagues and storm planning in one place. Every alliance that uses the bot can use it.

Open the LW Alliance Helper app

Getting started

  1. Leadership links your Discord server once. Run /vs and select "⚙️ Planner setup" → "🔗 Link this server", then enter your warzone number, alliance tag and alliance name.
  2. Members go to app.lwalliancehelper.com and select Sign in with Discord. The app finds their alliance from their roles in your Discord server.

For members

  • Alliance Duel (VS): a plan for each day of the week, so you know what to use and when. Check in each day with what you used and your score.
  • Game profile: your heroes, research, speed-ups and inventory, which your plan is built from. Fill in each section by hand. 💎 With Premium, upload screenshots of your game and we fill in your game profile from them.

For leadership

  • Scores: upload a screen recording or screenshots of the alliance ranking, and we fill in each member's daily VS score for you to check. The alliance's day totals go to your Sheet through the bot.
  • Start a new league: add the Duel League screenshots or a screen recording, check the alliances we find, and the league goes to your Sheet through the bot.
  • Desert Storm and Canyon Storm: build each team on the map, save templates to your Sheet, look back at past rosters, and add results from screenshots.
  • Roster and Growth: your members with their power and storm attendance, and how your alliance is growing.

Posts in Discord

The bot can post inventory reminders for members and a daily progress post for leadership. Both stay off until leadership turns them on in /vs → "⚙️ Planner setup".

Your data

A screen recording stays on your device. The app cuts it into images there and sends only the images it needs to scan, and those images aren't kept. The app has its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which cover what it keeps and how to remove it.
