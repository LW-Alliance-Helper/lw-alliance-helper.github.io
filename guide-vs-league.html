Start an Alliance Duel league

Use this guide while starting a new Alliance Duel league. Gather the details from the game, then return to Discord to enter them.

1. The League screen

In the game, open Alliance Duel → Duel League.
1. The season.
2. The tier and group.
3. Every alliance's tag, in ranking order. Scroll to see all 16.

2. An alliance's page

Tap an alliance in the list.
1. The alliance tag
2. Power, gift level and members. These are optional, and you can add them later.
3. Tap Members to find its warzone.

3. The member list

1. Tap any member. The leader at the top is the easiest to find.

4. The warzone

1. The number after # is the alliance's warzone.
Go back and repeat steps 2 to 4 for each alliance.

What to type

One alliance per line, in ranking order, as the game shows it:
<Glo> #999 26,800,000,000 Lv.25 95/100
Only the tag and warzone are required: Glo 999.

Shortcut: let an AI tool type it

Give your screenshots to any AI tool that reads images, with this request:
List every alliance in these screenshots, one per line, in ranking order: tag, warzone, power, gift level, members. Copy each value exactly as shown.

Check each tag against the League screen before you paste. Look-alike letters, like l and I, make one alliance look like two.

Then

Select Start a new league, enter the season, tier and group from step 1, and paste your list.
